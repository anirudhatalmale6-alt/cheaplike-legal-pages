Refund and Refill Policy

Last updated: [DATE]

This Refund and Refill Policy explains when refunds, refills and cancellations are available for Services purchased on cheaplike.net. It forms part of our Terms of Service.

Please read this policy before placing an order. Our Services are digital and begin processing immediately, so the rules below are important.

1. Summary

  • Orders that have not started can normally be cancelled and refunded to your account balance.
  • Orders that are completed are not refundable, because the Service has been fully delivered.
  • Orders that are partially delivered are refunded for the undelivered portion.
  • Orders that fail completely are refunded in full to your account balance.
  • Services marked "Refill" or "Guaranteed" are covered by the refill terms in Section 5.

2. Digital services and immediate delivery

All Services sold on this Website are digital marketing services that are sent to our suppliers and begin processing as soon as the order is placed. At checkout you expressly request immediate performance and acknowledge that once a Service has been fully performed your statutory right to cancel no longer applies. This does not affect your other legal rights, including your right to a remedy if a Service is not supplied with reasonable care and skill.

3. Cancellations

  1. You may request cancellation of an order while its status is Pending and it has not yet started. Approved cancellations are refunded to your account balance.
  2. Once an order status is In Progress, Processing or Completed, it cannot be cancelled, because the work has already been sent to the supplier and cannot be recalled.
  3. We cannot cancel an order because you feel it is taking longer than expected, provided it is still within the estimated delivery window shown on the Service listing.

4. When we do issue a refund

We will refund your order to your account balance in the following cases:

  1. Non-delivery. The order failed entirely and nothing was delivered.
  2. Partial delivery. Only part of the ordered quantity was delivered. We refund the value of the undelivered portion.
  3. Service unavailable. We cancel the Service on our side, or the Service is discontinued before your order is delivered.
  4. Duplicate charge. You were charged twice for the same payment due to a technical error.
  5. Our error. We delivered a materially different Service from the one you ordered.

5. Refill and guarantee

  1. A refill is only available where the Service name or description states "Refill" or "Guarantee" and specifies a period (for example "Refill 30 days").
  2. Services that do not state a refill period are not covered by any refill or drop guarantee, even in the event of a full drop.
  3. To be eligible for a refill, the request must be made within the stated refill period, and the current count must be below the count recorded on completion of the order and above the count recorded when the order started.
  4. A refill restores up to the quantity originally purchased. We do not provide unlimited refills unless expressly stated on the Service listing.
  5. A refill is provided instead of, not in addition to, a refund.

6. When we cannot refund or refill

Refunds and refills are not available where:

  1. the target account was set to private, was restricted, or was made private at any point during delivery;
  2. the username or link was changed after the order was placed;
  3. the target post, video or account was deleted, hidden or removed by you or by the platform;
  4. an incorrect link, username or Service was submitted by you;
  5. you placed multiple orders for the same link at the same time, which we cannot separate or verify;
  6. you used another provider on the same link at the same time;
  7. the order was delivered as described and you are dissatisfied with the quality of a Service that carried no guarantee;
  8. the social media platform removed engagement after the refill period had expired;
  9. the Service listing expressly stated that no refund or refill applies.

7. How refunds are paid

  1. Refunds are normally credited to your account balance on the Website, so that you can use them for other Services immediately.
  2. Account balance credit is not withdrawable as cash by default. It remains available for use on the Website.
  3. Where you are legally entitled to a refund to your original payment method, we will make that refund to the original payment method used, within 14 days of accepting the claim. Refunds cannot be sent to a different card, wallet or account from the one used to pay.
  4. Payment provider or network fees, and any losses caused by cryptocurrency price movement between payment and refund, are not refundable.
