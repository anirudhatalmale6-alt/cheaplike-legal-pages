## 2. Before you publish — fill in every placeholder

Search each file for `[` and replace. Nothing in square brackets should remain.

- `[YOUR FULL LEGAL NAME]` — your legal name as registered with HMRC
- `[YOUR BUSINESS ADDRESS]` — your trading address
- `[YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL / TICKET LINK]` — a working support contact
- `[YOUR TICKET LINK]` — your panel's support ticket URL
- `[DATE]` — the date you publish
- `[LINK TO REFUND POLICY PAGE]` / `[LINK TO PRIVACY POLICY PAGE]` — real URLs on your site
- `[YOUR CURRENCY]`, support hours, response times, refund window — your real values
- `[LIST YOUR ACTIVE METHODS]` — only list methods you actually have live

**Important:** the support email must actually work and be monitored. Reviewers do test it.

## 3. How to publish on Perfect Panel

Admin panel → **Pages** (or *Content* / *Custom pages*) → **Add page** for each file.

- Paste the HTML into the page editor in **HTML / source mode** (not visual mode).
- Set clean URLs, e.g. `/terms`, `/refund`, `/privacy`, `/contact`.
- **Link all four in your site footer.** They must be reachable *without logging in* — a reviewer will not have an account. This is critical and often missed.

## 5. Notes on how these were written

- **"No refunds" is not usable as a blanket rule in the UK.** A flat no-refund clause on a consumer site is unenforceable and gateway reviewers reject it. What *is* both legal and industry standard — and what these pages do — is:
  - orders **not yet started** → cancellable
  - orders **partially delivered** → refund the undelivered part
  - orders **fully delivered as described** → no refund
  - **Refill/Guarantee** services → covered for the stated period only
  - refunds go to **account balance** by default

  This gives effectively the same commercial outcome as the industry norm, while staying lawful and approvable.

- The 14-day cancellation right is handled the correct UK way: the customer **expressly requests immediate performance** at checkout and acknowledges losing the right to cancel once performed. For this to hold, add a checkbox at checkout:

  > *"I request that my order begins immediately and I understand I lose my right to cancel once it is fully delivered."*

- Refund rules follow SMM industry practice (private accounts, username changes, deleted posts, concurrent orders all void guarantees), so you are not giving away more than competitors do.

- The chargeback section asks customers to contact you first. This wording helps in disputes and gateways like to see it.
